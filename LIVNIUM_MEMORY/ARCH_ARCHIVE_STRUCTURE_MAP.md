directory. Repetitive or third-party leaves are represented by location and
count:

  including 1,010 CNFs;

  metadata in the v5 copy; and

architecture beneath 1,500 repetitive data filenames.

## 11. Architectural reading

The tree is not one monolithic project. It has seven distinct structural
zones:

1. **Base implementation:** `core/`.
2. **Geometry variants:** `core-c/`, `core-o/`, `core-t/`.
3. **Applications and scientific trials:** `experiments/`.
4. **Reusable evaluation corpora/harnesses:** `benchmark/`.
5. **Frozen external inputs:** `market-killer/market` and `brain/emb_cache`.
6. **Notation and speculative design:** `language/` and `important.md`.
7. **Historical presentation and placeholders:** top-level PNGs, `logs/`,
   and `luge/`.

The correct preservation unit is therefore the root plus its explicit zones,
not a forced merge into the current Core and not three independent copies.
