## Executive verdict

The 40-file Governance/Economy family is one ten-experiment lineage copied
exactly between the organized folder and the archive root. It develops a
recognizable sequence:

1. an energy-and-favor election economy;
2. unelectable judges that prune anomalous candidates;
3. the 50% failure boundary of report-only consensus;
4. oracle, stake, and structural anchors;
5. expected-payoff deterrence through detection, slashing, and stake;
6. shared-fate payoff coupling;
7. ten-strike deletion and optional silence;
8. sparse “same-layer” reality sensors;
9. a scalar cube-rotation selector; and
10. an equivariant vector-field decoder.

The archive contains real ideas, real failure discovery, and several correct
mathematical components. It does **not** yet contain a working autonomous
governance system. Most successes arise because truth, honest reliability,
detection probability, or the correct structural subspace is supplied directly
by the experiment.

The strongest surviving results are:

- **Report-only median consensus has a 50% coordinated-contamination
  boundary.** This is a correct standard robust-statistics boundary and the
  source demonstrates it cleanly.
- **The incentive equations are valid conditional design equations.** A
  one-shot lie with exogenous detection probability `q` needs an unlimited-stake
  penalty multiplier of `1/q`; finite repeated play changes the exact threshold
  to about `1.0091` at `q=.15`, horizon 40.
- **The scalar and vector Reynolds operators are exact projectors.** The scalar
  rotation-invariant space has rank 21 of 343; the equivariant vector space has
  exact rank 42 of 1,029.
- **Distributed sensing is materially better conditioned than a concentrated
  sensor block for the constructed ten-mode field.** Forty random sensors have
  full rank 10 with median condition number 2.73; the chosen 40-cell block has
  rank only 6.
- **The original economy honestly records its own capture.** It elects all 26
  loud liars and is exactly as bad as “richest wins.” That negative result is
  useful.

The main claims that must be narrowed are:

- **The election economy does not use information quality.** Its `obs` argument
  is never read inside the election dynamics. Reversing every observation leaves
  the winner and every trajectory exactly unchanged.
- **The judge community is one global anomaly formula repeated with noise.**
  Judge identity, local observation, history, and opinion are unused. More
  judges average artificial noise; they do not contribute independent evidence.
- **The anchored majority result is an oracle construction.** One value sampled
  directly around the true answer already reduces 70%-cartel error from 4.97 to
  about 0.027. The stake simulation does not establish that such an anchor has
  “proved” honesty.
- **Shared fate is a payoff definition, not simulated governance.** The negative
  payoff follows algebraically because the experiment inserts
  `kappa * global_error * retained_share` as a loss. There is no agent choice,
  adaptation, dumping process, coalition response, or dynamic equilibrium.
- **The purge outcomes are transition assumptions.** Majority detection is set
  to exactly zero without an anchor, while every public deletion directly turns
  remaining liars honest with a stipulated probability.
- **“Staggered silence” contains no staggering or time.** One fixed mask removes
  the oracle-known noisiest voters in every replicate; the 95% failure is a
  hardcoded `9.99` score when only two voters remain.
- **Same-layer reality is sparse low-rank sensing, not one read-only truth pin at
  every cell.** Reports are unused, only 40 of 343 cells are sampled, the decoder
  knows the exact ten-dimensional generating basis, and the corruption branch
  has a cell-index alignment bug.
- **The selector win is a correct matched-projection result, not a natural
  selector at every level.** The clean signal is generated in the exact
  21-dimensional space onto which the method projects. The saved 0.248 error is
  essentially the analytic projected-white-noise value 0.2474.
- **Equivariant vectors supply a strong known prior, not universal
  error-correction.** There are 21 cell orbits of sizes 1, 6, 8, 12, and 24—not
  24 independent copies for every cell. An equivariant coordinated lie lies in
  the valid subspace and remains indistinguishable.

The best honest synthesis is:

> The lineage is a mechanism-design notebook. It correctly discovers that
> consensus, wealth, judges, punishment, and geometry each relocate rather than
> abolish trust. Its reusable core is a set of conditional boundaries and known
> projectors. The missing experiment is an end-to-end adversarial system in
> which truth access, detection, stake, judge corruption, participation, and
> information quality are all explicit and none is silently oracle-supplied.

Nothing in this family should be deleted. Strong components should be retained
as lemmas or baselines; overstated verdicts should remain as historical evidence
with the calibrated interpretation recorded here.

## Generation A — cube economy

### What it builds

The 343 cube cells are divided into 27 directional sign regions. Each region has
one scalar truth. Every region with at least three cells receives one high-energy
liar whose report is displaced by `±4`.

Each election begins with flat affinities. Voting power is energy plus favor.
Voters court candidates in proportion to candidate power. The winner repays its
backers, reciprocal affinities are increased, and the winner accrues more
energy. Favor, affinity, and energy are renormalized after each round.

### Saved result

| Measurement | Saved result |
|---|---:|
| Regions containing loud liar | 26 |
| Loud liars elected | 26/26 |
| Flat-average reconstruction error | 0.8344 |
| Energy-only reconstruction error | 4.6818 |
| Economy reconstruction error | 4.6818 |
| Winner stability after round one | 1.0 |
| “Cooperation” metric | 1.0368 → 243.7582 |
| Favor Gini | 0.2638 → 0.8604 |

The source correctly calls this a failure: wealth captures the economy.

### Fresh mechanism boundary

`obs` is a formal argument to `run_region`, but it has zero load references in
the function. It affects only the post-election score in `main`.

A fresh eight-member region was run with one observation vector and then with
that vector reversed. Both runs:

- elected candidate 0;
- produced exactly the same cooperation trajectory;
- produced exactly the same Gini trajectory;
- produced exactly the same stability trajectory; and
- ended with cooperation metric 63.99 and favor Gini 0.875.

The election cannot clean information it never reads.

The exploding reciprocal-affinity metric is not sufficient evidence of
cooperation. The update installs reciprocal support by construction, normalizes
mean affinity to one, and concentrates weight around the already dominant
winner. It measures reciprocal concentration under this rule, not independently
chosen cooperation, reciprocity under temptation, or welfare.

### Verdict

Preserve this as a clear **oligarchic capture counterexample**. Do not call it an
information economy or emergent cooperative cleaner until candidate information
quality can affect behavior and agents can choose among cooperation, defection,
abstention, and exit.

## Generation B — economy plus judges

### Actual mechanism

Roughly one-third of honest cells are randomly labeled judges and made
unelectable. At every round:

1. candidate consensus is `median(obs[cand])`;
2. anomaly is absolute distance from that consensus;
3. every judge generates the same anomaly vector multiplied by independent
   noise;
4. the average anomaly vote decays candidate energy, favor, and incoming
   affinity.

Judge identity `_j` is never read inside the loop. A judge has no local evidence,
reliability, model, stake, history, or opinion. Judges cannot be corrupt.

### Saved result

| Judge strength | Liars elected | Error | Final Gini |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.00 | 20/20 | 4.1064 | 0.9185 |
| 0.25 | 20/20 | 4.1064 | 0.7704 |
| 0.50 | 0/20 | 0.06895 | 0.9185 |
| 0.85 | 0/20 | 0.06748 | 0.9185 |
| Flat averaging | — | 0.36365 | — |

With one outlier displaced by four and honest noise only 0.08, a normalized
median-deviation filter predictably isolates the liar once pruning strength
crosses the constructed transition.

### Verdict

The result supports an **unelectable median-outlier monitor** in an
honest-majority region. It does not support a neutral judge community, resistance
to judge corruption, or independent community evidence. The high final Gini and
“cooperation” values also do not prove that concentration has become socially
honest; they show that the same feedback loop now concentrates around a
candidate left after filtering.

## Generation C — majority capture

### Verified boundary

For 40 scalar reports with a coordinated shifted cluster:

- the mean degrades continuously;
- the median flips at 50% contamination;
- incoherent symmetric noise can leave the median near truth even at high
  contamination; and
- a coordinated majority controls any report-only median.

Saved coordinated-cartel median error is 0.135 at 45%, 2.546 at 50%, 4.932 at
55%, and 4.972 at 70%. The exact behavior at 50% depends on the even-sample
median convention and noise, but the 50% breakdown statement is standard and
correct.

### What “anchored” means here

The experiment generates anchor values as:

```text
V_TRUE + HONEST_NOISE * random_normal
```

It then retains only reports within `LIE_SHIFT/2` of the anchor median. The
threshold is 2.5 while the cartel displacement is 5.

Fresh matched 6,000-repetition results at a 70% cartel:

| Trusted anchors | Mean anchored error |
|---:|---:|
| 0 | 4.9720 |
| 1 | 0.02716 |
| 3 | 0.02754 |
| 5 | 0.02743 |

One truth-centered value supplies almost the full result. Additional anchors
mainly average its small noise.

### Verdict

The experiment demonstrates the identifiability lesson:

> Arbitrary coordinated majority contamination cannot be resolved from the
> reports alone without extra assumptions or information.

It does not demonstrate how a real system obtains an unforgeable truth sample.
The prose suggestion that anchors could be “proven honest by surviving stake”
is not connected to the stake experiment and would not by itself prove that
their current value is true.

## Generation D — anchor showdown

### Oracle anchor

At 70% cartel, 40 social reports contain 12 honest and 28 cartel values. An
anchor at truth with weight `lambda` balances the ideal point masses at:

```text
12 + lambda = (40 + lambda) / 2
lambda = 16
```

The source states that truth holds iff `lambda > 16`. Its weighted-median
implementation uses `searchsorted` with the lower endpoint at equality. The
fresh exact boundary is therefore:

| Anchor weight | Source tie-break output |
|---:|---:|
| 15.0 | 5 |
| 15.999999 | 5 |
| 16.0 | 0 |
| 16.000001 | 0 |
| 17.0 | 0 |

At exactly 16 the mathematical median is set-valued over the gap; the
implementation chooses truth. The strict `>` claim should be written as:

> Above 16 truth uniquely dominates; at 16 the result depends on the median
> tie convention.

### Stake anchor

The source does not model reputation formation. It simply assigns cartel
weights:

- fresh cartel: 0.05;
- patient cartel: 1.00, equal to honest.

The saved errors—0.030 versus 4.969—are weighted-median consequences of those
assigned weights. This is a useful patience/Sybil warning, not evidence that the
reputation mechanism detects honesty.

### Structural anchor

The source sets `ref=0`, exactly the true value, and rejects reports more than
`tau=2` away. It survives the lie at 5 and accepts the lie at 1.5. This correctly
shows that a tolerance band bounds accepted displacement **if the reference is
correct**. It does not discover the structural reference.

### Stack claim

Oracle, stake, and structural components are run separately. No source composes
them, tests correlated failure, varies oracle quality, models reputation
formation, or gives an attacker a joint budget. “Stack them so all must fail” is
a proposal, not a result.

### Verdict

Preserve the taxonomy:

- oracle failure: corrupt or bias external truth;
- stake failure: patient or wealthy adversary;
- structural failure: valid in-tolerance attack;
- consensus failure: coordinated majority.

This is one of the family’s best conceptual artifacts. Keep it as a threat-model
matrix, not proof of a robust stack.

## Generation E — lie deterrence

### Exact process

A liar gains `gain` each round. Detection occurs independently with exogenous
probability `q`. If caught after `t` rounds, the slash is:

```text
min(penalty * t * gain, stake)
```

If not caught within the horizon, the liar keeps all accumulated gain.

This process has a closed-form finite expectation; Monte Carlo is unnecessary.

### Fresh exact thresholds with unlimited stake

| Detection `q` | One-shot threshold | 40-round threshold | 40-round escape |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1.00 | 1.0000 | 1.0000 | 0 |
| 0.40 | 2.5000 | 1.00000002 | `1.34e-9` |
| 0.15 | 6.6667 | 1.00910961 | 0.001502 |

“Almost surely caught” is an asymptotic statement. At finite horizon 40 the
escape probability is small, not zero, and the exact multiplier must be slightly
above one at `q=.15`.

### Stake cap

For the saved one-shot example `gain=20`, `q=.10`, `penalty=100`, exact expected
net is:

```text
20 - 0.1 * min(2000, stake)
```

| Stake | Exact expected net |
|---:|---:|
| 2 | 19.8 |
| 5 | 19.5 |
| 10 | 19.0 |
| 20 | 18.0 |
| 40 | 16.0 |
| 80 | 12.0 |
| 200 | 0.0 |

The source correctly shows that every tested stake through 80 fails. For
expected one-shot deterrence, stake 200 is sufficient even though
`penalty * gain = 2000`. Therefore:

- `stake >= gain/q` is the exact break-even cap for this one-shot setup;
- `stake >= penalty * worst-case gain` is sufficient to implement the uncapped
  fine, but is not generally necessary for expected deterrence.

### Verdict

The conditional incentive math survives. What remains unmodeled:

- where `q` comes from;
- false positives and honest-agent costs;
- correlated or strategic detection;
- changing behavior after observations;
- risk preference and limited liability;
- bankruptcy, exit, identity reset, or Sybil replacement;
- whether detected harm can be valued correctly; and
- integration with the archived judge mechanism.

Phrase the result as a design constraint under assumed `q`, not “then no one
ever gains by lying.”

## Generation F — shared fate

### Actual experiment

For a capturing cartel, the code defines:

```text
liar_net = G - kappa * Ecurve[p] * retained
```

with `G=1` and captured consensus error near `W=5`. The theoretical coupling is
therefore `G/W=.2`. On the saved noisy grid, exact per-point thresholds range
from 0.20267 at 55% cartel to 0.20015 at 95%; a 0.01 sweep reports 0.21.

At 70% cartel and `kappa=.4`, saved net payoff changes with retained share:

| Retained share | Saved net |
|---:|---:|
| 1.00 | -0.9889 |
| 0.50 | 0.0056 |
| 0.25 | 0.5028 |
| 0.10 | 0.8011 |
| 0.00 | 1.0000 |

### What survives

The experiment captures a valid mechanism-design lesson:

> A harm-coupled asset deters an attack only to the extent that the attacker
> cannot shed or hedge the harmed exposure before settlement.

The offloading boundary is worth retaining.

### What is not established

The claimed closure is tautological at the simulation level: a sufficiently
large loss term is inserted into the payoff, so the net becomes negative.
There are no agents and no equilibrium. The apparent independence from cartel
size arises because per-liar gain is fixed and consensus error is approximately
five for every `p>.5`.

A real test needs endogenous holdings, timing, shorting/hedging, exit, side
payments, heterogeneous values, measurement error, and strategic response.

## Generation G — ten-strike purge and silence

### Purge

The source gives each liar an independent per-round detection chance until ten
strikes. Two outcome transitions are explicit:

```text
if liar majority and no anchor: q_eff = 0
after D deletions:
    each remaining liar reforms with probability 1 - (1 - visibility)^D
```

At visibility 0.3, the direct reformation probability is:

| Simultaneous deletions | Reformation probability per remaining liar |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0.3000 |
| 2 | 0.5100 |
| 3 | 0.6570 |
| 5 | 0.8319 |
| 10 | 0.9718 |

Saved eradication therefore follows the transition rules:

| Scenario | Eradicated | Mean rounds | Final liar fraction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 30%, no anchor, visibility 0 | 100% | 55.0 | 0 |
| 30%, no anchor, visibility .3 | 100% | 39.4 | 0 |
| 70%, no anchor, visibility .3 | 0% | 300 | 0.70 |
| 70%, anchor, visibility .3 | 100% | 40.8 | 0 |

Public punishment does not cause observed deterrence; the model directly changes
liar identity from true to false after a deletion. Permanent deletion is also
modeled as removal with no replacement identity, appeal, cost, or false
positive.

### Silence

The source describes up to three rounds of rest and staggered participation, but
`silence_sweep` has:

- no time variable;
- no rounds;
- no cooldown;
- no previous-vote state;
- no schedule;
- no simultaneous-rest coordination; and
- no learned confidence.

It creates a vector containing the true noise scale of every voter, permanently
masks the noisiest fraction, and reuses that mask in every replicate. The result
that 60% silence is best follows because exactly 60% were assigned high noise.
At 95%, 39 of 41 are removed, leaving two below quorum three, and the code
inserts error `9.99`.

### Verdict

Preserve the two hypotheses—progressive discipline and confidence-aware
abstention—but retire the historical claim that public execution was observed
to deter or that staggered silence was implemented.

### Why the saved denoising number is expected

The clean “cube-style” signal is explicitly projected with this same operator.
Noise has per-coordinate standard deviation `1/sqrt(343)` and the clean signal
has unit norm. Orthogonal projection retains 21 of 343 isotropic noise
dimensions, so the analytic RMS error is:

```text
sqrt(21 / 343) = 0.2474358
```

The saved selector error is 0.248475. This close match shows the result is the
standard matched-subspace projection law.

| Random operator, generic data | 0.7660 | 0.8148 | 0.7886 |

The random-operator symmetric row confirms the source’s honest caveat: the gain
comes from the data being in the selected subspace, not from the cube Laplacian.

### Verdict

Preserve the projector and matched/mismatched-prior demonstration. Do not call it
nonlinear—it is a linear orthogonal projector—or a selector “at every level,”
because the script applies one fixed projector and has no hierarchy.

### Boundary

The claim of “24 copies per cell” is inaccurate. The same 21 scalar cell orbits
have sizes 1, 6, 8, 12, and 24. Stabilizers impose extra vector constraints; the
origin vector, for example, must be zero under all rotations.

More importantly, a real-valued linear subspace has no positive minimum distance
against arbitrary small in-subspace perturbations. An attacker can submit
another smooth equivariant field. The source itself acknowledges the global
equivariant-valid-lie limit.

### Verdict

Preserve this as a correct **known-subspace robust denoising prior** and one of
the stronger technical artifacts in the family. “Error-correcting code” is safe
only with the corruption model stated: large incoherent off-subspace errors. It
does not identify truth against coordinated in-subspace corruption.
