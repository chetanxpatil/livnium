Date: 2026-07-27

Status: incorporated historical root, including the material missed by the
earlier subtree audits.

This audit answers two separate questions:

1. Is `arch-archive` the oldest recovered Livnium work?
2. Did the earlier NLI, experiment, Core-O/Core-T, and benchmark audits cover
   everything inside it?

The answers are **no** and **no**.

`arch-archive` is an important late-2025 historical bundle, but the recovered

### 4.3 What survives

## 5. Core-C: clean circular engineering, no semantics yet

### 5.1 Inventory and tests

`core-c` contains six Python files and two Markdown specifications.

Because the on-disk folder name contains a hyphen, direct package pytest
collection fails. A temporary read-only symlink named `core_c` restores the
intended package boundary:

### 5.2 Verified scope

The implementation correctly provides:

- one center plus `N` equally spaced ring nodes;
- the cycle/radial adjacency declared by the model;
- two defined exposure classes;
- `SW=9f` and total `9N`;
- the cyclic action `C_N`;
- inverse rotation and angle restoration; and
- a small invariant ledger.

These are useful, transparent standard constructions.

### 5.3 Narrowed claims

- It is a geometric cycle scaffold, not yet a semantic engine: no text,
  meaning, learning, or downstream task enters the implementation.
- `encoding_base=N+1` is only a returned integer property. There is no
  sequence encoder/decoder proving the document's base-encoding law.
- Perfect rotation reversibility does not establish reversible payload
  encoding.
- `K_C=9` and `SW=9f` are definitions chosen by the specification, not
  discovered constants.

Core-C should be preserved as the cleanest small archived topology, but not
promoted as evidence for language understanding or new physics.

## 6. `market-killer`: valuable negative prototype

### 6.1 Inventory

The branch contains:

- 503 per-symbol OHLCV CSV files, about 456 MB total; and
- six Python scripts for loading, vectorizing, alignment/tension, regime
  thresholds, a constant sweep, and plotting.

The observations range from 1962-01-02 to 2023-09-21. The archive records no
download command, provider/version, constituent-date policy, adjustment
contract, or license. The symbol set is therefore a frozen dataset, not a
reproducible market benchmark.

`SPY.csv` is absent even though the default sweep requests SPY.

### 6.2 Implemented signal

Each day becomes:

- return;
- rolling 14-day return volatility;
- rolling volume z-score;
- high-low range divided by open; and
- close/open ratio.

The code globally standardizes each complete symbol history, compares the
current vector to an EMA of previous vectors, and defines:

`tension = abs(0.38 - cosine(current, prior_ema))`.

This is an angular novelty heuristic over standard technical features.

### 6.3 Fresh failures

The archived constant sweep reports `NaN` for
`corr(tension, |next_return|)` because it leaves the final shifted target as
`NaN` and passes the unmasked vector to `np.corrcoef`.

Across all 503 CSVs:

- 321 produce wholly finite state arrays;
- 182 contain non-finite values under the archived vectorizer, mainly from
  zero rolling volume standard deviations; and
- only 320 finite symbols also meet the bounded evaluation length.

After masking the shifted target solely to recover the intended statistic:

| Signal versus next absolute return | Mean symbol correlation | Median |
|---|---:|---:|
| Livnium tension | -0.0109 | -0.0097 |
| Current absolute return | 0.2495 | 0.2361 |
| Ordinary rolling 14-day volatility | 0.3536 | 0.3508 |

For the three explicitly rerun symbols:

| Symbol | Livnium tension | Current absolute return | Rolling volatility |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| AAPL | -0.0689 | 0.2025 | 0.2957 |
| TSLA | 0.0164 | 0.1295 | 0.2093 |
| MSFT | -0.0162 | 0.2415 | 0.3502 |

The Livnium statistic loses clearly to inputs already present in its own
feature vector.

Additional boundaries:

- whole-history standardization uses future distribution information;
- there is no train/validation/test split;
- no market-regime ground truth is defined;
- no trading rule, costs, slippage, turnover, drawdown, or out-of-sample
  backtest exists;
- the constant sweep does not optimize a valid metric; and
- with `C=0.38`, `alignment>=0.4` implies tension at most `0.62`, so the
  configured euphoria condition `tension>=0.8` is mathematically unreachable.

`market-killer` is incorporated as a reusable data snapshot and a strong
negative-control lesson. It is not evidence of prediction or trading alpha.

## 7. `important.md`: O-A8, O-A9, and O-A10

The 1,688-line document preserves three ambitious ideas.

### 7.1 O-A8 promotion

Surviving design idea:

> Evaluate a proposed hierarchy change transactionally and accept it only when
> a declared global objective improves.

That is a useful monotone-search/governance pattern. Keep:

- hypothetical evaluation before mutation;
- explicit cost and relief;
- donor/accounting ledgers;
- rejection when the declared objective worsens; and
- independent recomputation after application.

The archived implementation is illustrative Markdown only. No
`PromotionEngine`, `attempt_promotion`, or `remove_node` is integrated into
Core-O.

Its simulation equates promotion with removing a node from the parent shell.
Because most tension terms are nonnegative, removal normally lowers the
objective without proving that the node was usefully promoted, preserved
elsewhere, or improved. Donor energy is described but not implemented.

A monotonically decreasing scalar objective establishes neither:

- correctness of that objective;
- convergence to a global optimum;
- absence of local stagnation;
- preservation of useful diversity;
- “never collapses”; nor
- political or physical merit.

### 7.2 O-A9 infinite interior

Preserve as a multiscale-boundary research metaphor: a node may summarize a
finer internal system.

Retire the physical assertion. The document declares
`Phi_0 = lim Phi_n` without defining the maps, topology, contraction,
attenuation rate, or proving the limit exists. Similar vocabulary from
renormalization, holography, or spin networks is analogy, not structural
isomorphism or evidence about atoms.

### 7.3 O-A10 information condensation

Preserve as a testable hypothesis:

> Under a fixed resource/ledger budget, can increasing independent constraints
> improve a held-out task metric?

The stated law
`partial B / partial I > 0 when Delta(sum SW)=0` is not derived or measured.
`SW` stores geometric exposure, not Shannon information, so conserving it does
not prove that information or energy is conserved. The example code references
undefined operations and `previous_B`.

Most directly, the base core's fresh
`test_reinforcement_deepens` fails: 20 positive updates leave both curvature
and SW unchanged. O-A10 is therefore historical theory/hypothesis, not a law
implemented by this archive.

## 13. Carry-forward ideas

### AR-01 — Transactional promotion

Represent a proposed hierarchy change as an immutable candidate. Compute cost
and benefit before mutation, apply atomically, and independently verify the
post-state. The task metric—not metaphorical tension—must define benefit.

### AR-02 — Resource-fixed information-density test

At fixed parameter/memory/evaluation budgets, vary the number and independence
of constraints. Measure held-out quality, calibration, stability, and
compression. This turns O-A10 into a falsifiable curve rather than a law.

### AR-03 — Small cyclic reference frame

Use Core-C as a minimal testbed for phase/cycle tasks where cyclic symmetry is
actually present. Compare raw coordinates, Fourier features, a standard
equivariant baseline, and the proposed ledger under matched capacity.

### AR-04 — Market preflight

Before any market revival:

1. record data provider, exact download command, adjustment policy, and
   constituent date;
2. replace non-finite rolling features;
3. use train-only or causal scaling;
4. define the target independently;
5. compare return, rolling-volatility, momentum, and random baselines;
6. evaluate walk-forward after costs;
7. pre-register thresholds; and
8. save trades and recompute P&L independently.

### AR-05 — Provenance-bound figures

Every result figure must embed or accompany source hash, input/artifact hashes,
command, environment, seed, metric definition, and timestamp.
