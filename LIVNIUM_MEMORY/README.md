## The no-loss rule

Nothing is deleted merely because it looks old or duplicated.

A source may be retired only when:

1. it has a content hash proving it is an exact duplicate, or its unique material
   has been explicitly inventoried;
2. its concepts and results have been summarized in this memory;
3. its checkpoints, datasets, and commands have recorded locations;
4. its evidence status is marked in `CLAIM_LEDGER.md`; and
5. a recoverable archive still exists.

## Evidence vocabulary

- **Proven:** mathematical result with derivation and focused tests.
- **Verified engineering:** implementation behaves correctly, but is standard.
- **Measured:** empirical result with a recorded protocol.
- **Partial:** promising result missing controls, seeds, ablations, or replication.
- **Retired:** disproven, leaked, overstated, or superseded; kept as history.
- **Unreviewed:** discovered but not yet understood. Never discard this category.
