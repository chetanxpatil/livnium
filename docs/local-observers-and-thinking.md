# Thinking shapes thinking

*Research hypothesis — Chetan Patil*

Thinking shapes thinking.

Could self-preservation become a foundation for thinking?

When a system can detect threats to its continued existence, retain what happened, and change its actions accordingly, self-preservation can become a basis for thinking.

The important connection is between detection, memory and adaptation. What happens to the system changes how it responds the next time. It begins to distinguish what supports its continued existence from what threatens it.

Could AI develop more coherent behaviour when these processes work together, with local parts contributing to the continuity of the whole?

This is a hypothesis I’m exploring through LIVNIUM. It suggests one possible foundation for thinking, without assuming that self-preservation alone explains all thought or establishes subjective experience.
