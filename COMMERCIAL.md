# Commercial licensing

Livnium is **free for noncommercial use** under the PolyForm Noncommercial License
(see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). Individuals, students, researchers, hobbyists,
nonprofits, schools, and government institutions can use it at no cost.

**If you are a company and you want to use Livnium commercially, you need a paid
commercial license.** That includes, for example:

- Using Livnium (in whole or in part) in a product or service you sell or operate.
- Using it internally inside a for-profit company's operations or R&D pipeline.
- Bundling, embedding, or redistributing it as part of commercial software.
- Training, fine-tuning, or building commercial models or datasets on it.

There is **no open-source escape hatch and no race to the bottom** here on purpose:
the noncommercial license keeps it free for the curious, and commercial users pay.
That's the whole model.

## How it works

1. **Tell me what you want to do** — the product, the scale, roughly how Livnium
   fits in. A short email is fine.
2. **We agree on terms** — commercial licenses are typically a simple annual fee,
   scaled to company size and use. (Pricing is set per deal for now; it will be
   published here as it firms up.)
3. **You get a written commercial license** granting the rights the noncommercial
   terms don't — and you're covered.
